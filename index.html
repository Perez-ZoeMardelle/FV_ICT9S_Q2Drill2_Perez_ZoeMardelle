About OBMC

O.B. Montessori Center (OBMC) is a premier private, co-educational institution in the Philippines dedicated to providing a transformative, child-centered education. Founded in 1966 by Dr. Preciosa S. Soliven, OBMC pioneered the integration of Dr. Maria Montessori’s world-renowned holistic philosophy into the local curriculum. Through a nurturing environment that balances academic excellence with practical life skills, independence, and social responsibility, OBMC empowers students across five dynamic campuses to become self-reliant, disciplined global citizens and life-long learners.


Our Campuses

OBMC Fairview

Tailored to a thriving community of young suburban families in northern Metro Manila, the OBMC Fairview Campus delivers premium, modern Montessori education. The campus focuses heavily on student-led community outreach and collaborative programs, balancing rigorous academic preparation with holistic personal development.

Location
Belfast cor. Napoli Sts., Neopolitan IV, Brittany Subdivision, Fairview, Quezon City

OBMC Greenhills

OBMC Greenhills Campus offers a creative, high-energy environment designed to spark imagination, a love for lifelong learning, and community service. The multi-story facility features a signature open-air central courtyard for flag ceremonies and gatherings, preschool rooms designed with low shelves for self-directed learning, advanced science laboratories, and outdoor corridors populated with birds, turtles, and fish for active nature study.

Location
3 Eisenhower Street, Greenhills, San Juan City

OBMC Las Piñas

Serving the southern Metro Manila area, the OBMC Las Piñas Campus deliberately pays homage to the historical character of its city—the birthplace of the historic Sarao jeepney and the 19th-century Bamboo Organ. It features a unique welcoming atmosphere and is notable for hosting dedicated self-sufficiency agricultural and farming spaces that teach young learners independent lifestyle skills and environmental stewardship early on.

Location
Luxembourg Street, B.F. Homes, Las Piñas City

OBMC Sta. Ana

OBMC Sta. Ana Campus centers its student identity around historical culture, heritage preservation, and deep community involvement. It balances a dense, urban campus layout with highly praised academic curriculum tracks and localized civic projects.

Location
2241 Pedro Gil Street, Sta. Ana, Manila City

OBMC Angeles

Situated in the culturally rich and vibrant province of Pampanga, the OBMC Angeles Campus provides a highly nurturing, dynamic Montessori environment. It is carefully structured to help provincial students thrive both academically and personally while maintaining strong ties to region-wide community initiatives.

Location
MacArthur Highway, Angeles City, Pampanga
